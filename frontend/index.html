About Spark IIT

A Legacy of Technical Competence & Vocational Excellence

Founded with the mission to bridge the digital skills gap, Spark IIT provides practical, job-ready computer education accessible to all learners.

Our Mission

To deliver rigorous, practical computer education that transforms aspiring students into skilled, industry-ready professionals through hands-on lab training and ethical mentorship.

Our Vision

To be a premier vocational institution recognized nationally for producing top-tier computer technicians, web creators, accountants, and software associates who drive digital growth.

Our History

Established over 15 years ago with a single 10-computer laboratory, Spark IIT has expanded into a full-scale vocational center having empowered more than 12,500 youth with career diplomas.
